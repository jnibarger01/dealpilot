Status: Approved · Owner: Founding GTM (launch) + CTO (quality bar) · Predecessor gate: `mvp.md` §6 success criteria met.

## 1. v1 Thesis

MVP proved one buyer in one market pays; v1 proves the machine **repeats without hand-holding**: self-serve activation, two wedges, sustainable unit economics, and the first proof that trust artifacts convert strangers (not just design partners we charmed personally).

## 3. Launch Plan (wk −4 → +4, from `../business/marketing.md` launch campaign)

Wk −4: waitlist warm-up via *State of Seller Claims* teaser data; design partners record proof stories. Wk −2: press/creator briefings under embargo (the tamper-the-ledger demo is the hook); status page + trust center live. **Wk 0:** launch post + verifier repo spotlight + founder demo video; community AMA in REI/ETA channels. Wk +1–4: SEO pillar pages ship; creator reviews drop staggered; weekly changelog rhythm starts (public, honest, includes UNKNOWN-rate stats — shipping our metrics is the marketing).
**Launch KPIs (45 d):** 2,000 signups → 300 activated (first verified memo) → 90 paid. Guardrail metrics watched equally: activation-to-aha ≤ 4 min p50, support load < 0.15 tickets/activation, COGS/memo ≤ $11.

## 4. Launch Readiness Checklist (G5 gate, `../engineering/testing.md` §3 — every line has an owner)

- [ ] Load: launch-spike scenario (5× projected day-1) passed twice consecutively
- [ ] On-call: rotation staffed, RB drills current, status page rehearsed
- [ ] Billing: dunning, refund policy, credit auto-refund on system-fault runs verified end-to-end with real cards
- [ ] Legal: ToS/Privacy final counsel pass; perimeter linter live on outputs (`../legal/compliance.md` §6)
- [ ] Quality: G4 gates green 14 consecutive days; injection corpus 100%; human-review queue SLA < 4 h at projected volume
- [ ] Trust: verifier v1.0 reproducible-build verified by an external volunteer; docs tested on a stranger
- [ ] Kill-switches: free-tier depth, signup throttle, run-concurrency caps — tested, not just present
- [ ] Analytics: funnel instrumented end-to-end *before* traffic (unmeasured launches teach nothing)

## 5. Post-Launch Iteration Themes (mo 7–12, re-prioritized biweekly against data)

1. **Activation friction** (biggest lever on every downstream number): time-to-first-memo, empty-state quality, sample-deal experience.
2. **UNKNOWN → resolution loop**: close-the-unknowns completion rate is the retention leading indicator (`../business/business-plan.md` retention system) — instrument, then iterate the worklist UX hard.
3. **CIM wedge quality**: SMB claim types will lag RE accuracy initially; honest per-wedge quality dashboards, reviewer staffing weighted accordingly.
4. **Cost curve**: $11 → $8 by mo 12 (routing v2, evidence cache hit-rate, batch adjudication) — tracked weekly, R-3 discipline.
5. **API alpha feedback** → GA spec freeze decision by mo 12.

## 6. Exit Criteria → Enterprise Phase (`enterprise.md`)

$600K ARR run-rate · activation ≥ 40% · NRR early-cohort signal ≥ 100% · auto-verification ≥ 70% held for a quarter · SOC 2 Type I in hand · ≥ 3 inbound team/enterprise-shaped conversations in pipeline (pull, not push — we enter the enterprise motion when the market drags us, per `../business/sales.md` sequencing rule). Miss on ARR but green on quality/activation → the mo-18 downshift trigger governs (`../business/financial-model.md`): freeze at 14 heads, extend runway, fix the growth constraint before scaling spend.
