# DealPilot — Capital Strategy & Use of Funds
Status: Approved · Owner: CEO · Round mechanics, target list, and process live in `../investor/fundraising.md`.

## 1. Financing Philosophy

Raise against **trust milestones**, not vanity growth. Each round is priced by a provable claim: Seed = "the doctrine works and people pay for it"; Series A = "unit economics of verification are sound and enterprises buy it"; Series B = "the platform is becoming the standard." We keep 24+ months of runway at all times and pre-agree downshift triggers with the board (see `financial-model.md` §4).

## 3. Series A — $15M (target mo 22–26)

Purchases: enterprise motion industrialization (AE pods, CSM, security surface), lender vertical entry, Evidence Graph scale-out, API GA. Dilution budget ≤22%.

## 4. Series B — $35M (target mo 44–50)

Purchases: marketplace launch, category marketing, international data expansion (UK/CA first — common-law records, English-language evidence). Contingent on NRR ≥120% enterprise and API partner traction.

## 5. Non-Dilutive & Alternatives Considered

- Venture debt: eligible post-A ($3–5M facility) as insurance, not plan-of-record.
- Revenue-based financing: rejected — margin profile too early, covenants misaligned with credit-price flexibility we need.
- Strategic investment (data provider or lender): considered at A only with clean information rights; no board seat, no exclusivity, no ROFR on company — a data-supplier investor with a ROFR is a moat leak.

## 6. Dilution Ledger (planning)

Founders post-seed ≈ 63% → post-A ≈ 47% → post-B ≈ 36% (with pool refreshes; detail in `../investor/cap-table.md`). Guardrail: founders + employees ≥50% through A.
