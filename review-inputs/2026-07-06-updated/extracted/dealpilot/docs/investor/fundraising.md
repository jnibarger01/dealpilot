# DealPilot — Fundraising Process & Round Mechanics
Status: Working playbook · Owner: CEO · Scope split: `../business/fundraising.md` covers capital *strategy* (why/when/how much, use of funds, dilution budget); this doc covers the *round itself* — targets, process, terms, closing mechanics.

## 1. Round Spec (Seed)

$4.0M priced Series Seed at $16M post (`cap-table.md` §2 for structure). Lead: $1.5–2.5M writer taking the board seat; 2–4 followers; ≤ $500K reserved for strategic angels (RE fund principals, ETA community operators, ex-appraisal-regulatory — people who *are* distribution). Min check $50K (angel SPV below that). No party rounds without a real lead — governance beats logo count.

## 2. Target Investor Profile (qualify hard, it's mutual)

Must-haves: seed fund with $150M–$600M AUM (our check matters to them; reserves exist for the A); thesis fit in AI-applied/vertical-SaaS/fintech-infra; **temperament for fail-closed products** — a fund that pushed a portfolio company toward engagement-metric theater would push us to weaken refusals; partner (not principal) sponsorship. Nice-to-haves: portfolio adjacency without conflict (diligence tools ok; a competing underwriting bet is a pass), in-house technical diligence capacity (our §4 due-diligence protocol lands best with funds who'll actually run the eval harness).

Anti-profile (documented so we don't rationalize mid-process): tourist AI capital chasing demos; funds requiring advice-flavored roadmap items ("just tell users buy/pass") — that's a perimeter violation dressed as PMF advice (`../legal/compliance.md` §6); strategic corporates wanting ROFR/data rights (rejected categorically per `../business/fundraising.md` §5).

## 3. Target List Construction

Build 40 names in three tiers (T1 dream-fit ×8, T2 strong ×18, T3 backstop ×14); source via: founders whose companies those funds led at seed (reference the *partner*, not the fund), design partners' investor networks, and funds that publicly wrote about verification/eval-quality theses. Sequencing: 4 T2 meetings first (calibrate narrative, burn nobody precious), then T1 in a tight 2-week window to synchronize partner meetings. Every meeting logged (CRM: stage, objections verbatim, next step, decay date) — pipeline discipline identical to `../business/sales.md` §6, because it is sales.

## 4. Process Timeline (target 6 weeks from first partner meeting)

| Week | Activity | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|
| −4 → 0 | Materials freeze (deck, model, data room T1), 3 mock partner meetings with friendly investors | narrative survives hostile Q&A |
| 1–2 | First meetings ×20–25 | ≥8 second meetings |
| 2–3 | Partner meetings, tech diligence sessions (we *offer* the eval-harness reproduction — `due-diligence.md` §4) | ≥3 funds in IC |
| 3–4 | Term sheets; run comparably, honestly ("we have interest, decision by [date]" — never fabricated heat) | signed TS |
| 4–6 | Confirmatory diligence, docs (NVCA standard, redline discipline: counsel flags non-standard only), close | wires |

Kill-switch: if week 3 yields zero ICs, stop, synthesize objections, fix the two loudest, re-approach T3 + new names in 60–90 days — a stalled raise broadcast widely is worse than a paused one. Runway rule from `../business/fundraising.md`: start every raise with ≥9 months cash so no one smells desperation.

## 5. Terms Guardrails (pre-committed walk-aways)

Accept: 1× non-participating, broad-based WA anti-dilution, pro-rata for majors, standard protectives, lead board seat. Negotiate: pool size/timing (we model pre-money 12%, `cap-table.md` §2), info rights scope. **Walk away**: participating preferred, >1× preference, full ratchet, redemption rights, investor veto on operating budget lines, tranched milestones on *product* metrics (milestone tranches convert doctrine into demo-pressure — the exact failure mode this company exists to avoid), ROFR/exclusive data rights for strategics. Valuation flexibility band: $14–18M post before structure; we trade price before we trade terms, always.

## 6. The Pitch-to-Close Asset List

Deck (`pitch-deck.md`) · 3-min recorded demo (tamper-the-ledger finale) · data room tiers (`due-diligence.md` §1) · model xlsx mirroring `../business/financial-model.md` with a scenario toggle (base/bear) · reference sheet (8 design partners, 4 personal references incl. one "hard time" reference we volunteer) · FAQ doc = `due-diligence.md` §3 verbatim (answers are pre-agreed so both founders say the same thing in parallel meetings — inconsistency is the #1 diligence red flag we can control).

## 7. Communication Doctrine During the Raise

Weekly investor-update cadence to engaged funds (ships Monday: metric deltas, one honest lowlight, asks); no exclusive information to any single fund (fairness = leverage); all forward-looking statements framed with the same discipline as product claims — a fund should notice that our *fundraising* passes the Manual Verifier Doctrine. Post-close: monthly update (KPIs vs plan, cash, hiring, risks moved on the register) within 5 business days of month end, forever — the update habit is cheaper than every alternative reputation strategy.

## 8. Series A Pre-Work (starts the day seed closes)

Maintain a living A-narrative doc (what must be true; gates from `../business/fundraising.md` §4 tracked monthly on the board pack's first page); quarterly coffee-not-pitch meetings with 6–8 A funds (relationship compounding — funds buy trajectories they've watched); data room stays continuously current (`due-diligence.md` preamble) so the A process is a 4-week sprint, not a 4-month archaeology dig.
